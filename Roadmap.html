The startup roadmap

Six steps, in the order most small businesses actually take them. You don't need to finish one perfectly before starting the next, but skipping step 2 is the most expensive mistake on this page.

The six steps

  1. 1

    Find a problem worth solving

    Start with something people already complain about, pay for, or work around. Write the problem in one sentence before you think about the product.

  2. 2

    Test demand before you build

    Talk to ten possible customers, put up a one-page site, or take pre-orders. If nobody will give you their email address, they won't give you money yet.

  3. 3

    Write a one-page plan

    Who is the customer, what do they pay, what does it cost you, and how will they find you? The government has a free business plan template if you want more structure.

  4. 4

    Choose a structure and register

    Most first businesses start as a sole trader because it is the cheapest and simplest structure. The table below compares the four main options.

    What most new businesses register

    • An ABN, which is free (business.gov.au, 2025).
    • A business name, unless you trade under your own name.
    • GST, once your turnover reaches $75,000 (Australian Taxation Office, no date).
      • Non-profits register at $150,000.
      • Taxi and ride-sourcing drivers register from their first dollar.
    Fees from 1 July 2026

    A business name costs $47 for one year or $108 for three years. Registering a company costs $636, plus a $342 annual review fee.

    Source: business.gov.au (2026)
  5. 5

    Launch small

    Sell to your first ten customers by hand. A market stall, an Instagram shop or a simple booking page is enough. You'll learn more from one awkward sale than a month of planning.

  6. 6

    Measure and grow

    Track three numbers each week: sales, cost per sale, and how many customers come back. Grow the thing that's working and drop the thing that isn't.

Which business structure?

Each structure changes who is responsible for debts, how much paperwork you do and what it costs (business.gov.au, 2024b). You can change structure later as the business grows.

Words you'll hear

Five terms that come up in almost every government form.

ABN
An 11-digit number that identifies your business to the government and other businesses.
ACN
A 9-digit number given to a company when it is registered with ASIC.
GST
A 10% tax on most goods and services sold in Australia.
BAS
The form a GST-registered business lodges to report and pay its GST and some other taxes.
MVP
The simplest version of your product that real customers can try, so you learn before you spend.

Costs and funding

Starting small can cost far less than people expect. Registering an ABN is free, and a business name is $47 for a year (business.gov.au, 2026).

An example first-year budget

Most of your first-year spending goes on running the business, not registering it. The example below is for a small online craft shop run by one person.

Example only. Amounts marked as estimates will be different for your business.
Category Item Cost (AUD)
Setting up ABN registration $0
Business name, one year $47
Domain name and website builder (estimate) $300
Running costs Public liability insurance (estimate) $600
Accounting and design software (estimate) $360
Small marketing tests (estimate) $500
Stock and tools Starter materials (estimate) $1,500
Equipment (estimate) $800
Example total for year one $4,107

Setting up as a company instead? Add $636 to register it and $342 each year for the annual review (business.gov.au, 2026).

Five ways to spend less in year one

  • Start as a sole trader and change structure when the business needs it.
  • Trade under your own name until you need a business name.
  • Use the free tier of software until you outgrow it.
  • Sell before you stock: take pre-orders for your first batch.
  • Borrow or buy second-hand equipment.

Please note: this page is general information, not financial advice. Talk to an accountant or a registered tax agent before making money decisions.
